# ⚔️ Battle Arena: Clash of Sorcerors — 4K Ultra 3D Edition

An adrenaline-pumping **4K Ultra 3D Open World fighting arena game** featuring 16 legendary characters from **Jujutsu Kaisen**, ranked and balanced according to their canonical power levels, grades, and domain expansions. Built with **Three.js (WebGL 3D)**, hardware-accelerated procedural 4K textures, 4096x4096 soft PCF shadows, procedural Web Audio synthesis, and dynamic combat camera tracking.

---

## 🌟 Key 4K Ultra Features

### ✨ True 4K Ultra High-DPI Engine Pipeline
* **High-DPI Scaler**: Dynamically scales up to native 4K UHD (3840x2160) and Retina displays with supersampled pixel ratios.
* **Always-On 4K Ultra Pipeline**: Permanently enabled 4K Ultra supersampled high-DPI rendering across all displays.
* **Procedural 4K Textures**: 2048x2048 weathered stone paving tiles, inlaid gold Jujutsu seals, authentic Heian cursed tattoo maps, and woven silk kimono cloth patterns.
* **4096x4096 Soft Shadows & HDR Tone Mapping**: Real-time PCF soft contact shadows, ACESFilmic high dynamic range tone mapping, and cool rim lighting highlighting fighter silhouettes.
* **Razor-Sharp 4K HUD**: High-DPI vector health bars, glowing cursed energy gauges, combo counter, and floating damage typography.

### ⛩️ Sorcerer Sign-In & Custom Identity System
* **Sorcerer Sign-In on Entry**: Players register their custom Sorcerer Name, Grade & Title (*Special Grade, Grade 1, Tokyo Jujutsu Tech, Curse User*), and choose an Affinity Crest avatar (*Six Eyes, Curse King, Black Flash, Shadow Wolf, Blood Convergence, Cursed Tool*).
* **Live In-Game Identity**: Your personalized Sorcerer Name and title appear on the Battle HUD, the top profile pill, the main menu profile card, and victory/defeat screens.
* **Easy Profile Switcher**: Click your profile chip anytime to update your name or switch identities.

### 🤝 Play vs Friend Mode
* **Local Split Duel**: Challenge a friend side-by-side on the same keyboard with full dual 3D combat controls, custom friend naming, and independent character picking.
* **Laptop-Friendly Controls**: Friends on laptops without a numeric keypad can use `↑ ↓ ← →` movement with `I` (Jump), `O` (Attack), `P` (Skill 1), `[` (Skill 2), `]` (Ultimate), and `.` or `/` (Block).
* **Shareable Challenge Invite**: Generate match room codes and shareable challenge invite links (`?mode=friend&challenger=YourName&room=JJK-XXXX`) to invite friends on chat, WhatsApp, or Discord.

### 🎥 Dynamic 3D Combat Camera System
Toggle between 3 distinct camera modes at any time with the `V` key or top-bar button:
1. **Action Orbit Cam (Default)**: Cinematic camera dynamically tracking the midpoint between both fighters in 3D space, intelligently zooming in during close melee combat and pulling back when fighters separate across the open arena.
2. **Third-Person Cam (Follow)**: Over-the-shoulder action RPG perspective following behind Player 1.
3. **Overview Cam (Bird's Eye)**: High-angle aerial view for tactical arena positioning.
* Features responsive camera shake during heavy hits, Black Flash critical strikes, and Domain Expansions!

## 🚀 How to Run

### Option 1: Quick Launch
Double-click `play_game.bat` in the repository root to launch immediately in your default browser.

### Option 2: Browser
Open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
* Works 100% offline via local `js/lib/three.min.js` and includes automatic CDN fallback!
