🪙 Fair Point Economy System

There are no infinite coin cheats or exploits. Players earn Battle Points through combat skill:

  • Starting Balance: 0 Points (earn every coin through combat victories).
  • Match Victory: 300 to 600 Points (scales with Easy, Medium, Hard, Deity).
  • Combo Streaks: Up to +200 bonus Points for chaining hits.
  • Time Bonus: Earn bonus points for fast victories.
  • Arcade Ladder: Defeat Zeus, Anubis, Wukong, and Sukuna in order to claim the Grand Pantheon Purse!
  • Safe Storage: Unlocked deities and points persist in your browser's local storage.
